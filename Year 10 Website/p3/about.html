About the Kiwi

Kiwis are small, flightless birds with hair‑like feathers and powerful legs. They use their long beaks (with nostrils at the tip!) to sniff out insects, worms, and berries.

Quick Facts

  • Habitat: Native forests and scrub in New Zealand
  • Diet: Invertebrates, worms, fruits
  • Fun: Lay one of the largest eggs relative to body size

Conservation

Predator control and protected sanctuaries are crucial for kiwi survival. Dogs, stoats, and cats pose major threats to eggs and chicks.
