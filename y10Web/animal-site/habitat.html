Habitat

Red pandas live in temperate forests with dense bamboo, usually at elevations between 2,200–4,800 m in the Himalayas and nearby mountain ranges.

Range

Found in Nepal, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, and China—preferring cool, moist climates with plenty of tree cover and bamboo understory.

Adaptations

  • Thick fur and bushy tail for warmth.
  • Semi‑retractable claws for climbing.
  • Excellent balance on narrow branches.
