Red Panda

The red panda is a small mammal from the eastern Himalayas and southwestern China, known for its fluffy tail and tree‑climbing skills.

Diet

Red pandas primarily eat bamboo shoots and leaves. They also snack on berries, flowers, acorns, and occasionally insects or bird eggs.

  • They are technically carnivores but mostly eat plants.
  • They can spend up to 13 hours a day feeding.
  • They use their wrist bone like a thumb to grasp bamboo.

Quick Facts

  • Scientific name: Ailurus fulgens
  • Status: Endangered
  • Average length: ~60 cm (plus tail)
